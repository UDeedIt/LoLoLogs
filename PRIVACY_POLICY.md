# Privacy Policy for LoLo Logs Library Demo

Effective Date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the LoLo Logs Demo ("the App"), developed by UDeedIt Pro, handles data and logging. The App is a professional demonstration utility for the LoLo Logs Library, designed to showcase lightweight, high-performance, and cross-platform (KMP) logging capabilities for the D2D ecosystem.

## 1. Zero Data Collection Policy
LoLo Logs is built on a "Privacy-First" architecture. We do not collect, transmit, or store any user data, application logs, or telemetry on external servers. All logging operations are performed locally and output exclusively to the host device's native logging system (e.g., Logcat for Android, os_log for iOS).

## 2. Local Logging Rationale
The App serves as a live environment to test the library's ability to format, structure, and output logs efficiently. As such:
*   **Demonstration Logs:** The App will generate sample logs (Debug, Info, Warn, Error) to demonstrate the library's zero-allocation performance and Flow integration.
*   **No Remote Access:** Even when detailed structured logs are generated, the App does not export, share, or upload these logs to any external endpoints.
*   **Volatile Output:** The library acts strictly as a conduit to the system's native log buffers, which are managed entirely by the host operating system.

## 3. Third-Party Integration
This App is a pure utility. It does not contain third-party tracking, advertising, crash reporting, or analytics SDKs. No information regarding your device or logging patterns is shared with third-party service providers.

## 4. Developer & User Transparency
As a tool designed to make cross-platform debugging effortless, we believe in complete control. The logging levels and outputs can be dynamically controlled, filtered, or entirely disabled by the implementing developer without affecting the App's stability.

## 5. Data Deletion
Since LoLo Logs does not persist logs in localized files by default, there is no persistent app data to delete. All standard logs reside in the OS-level buffers and are routinely purged by the operating system or when the device is rebooted.

## 6. Commitment to Developers
As a D2D (Developer-to-Developer) product, we are committed to transparency. The logic demonstrated in this app is identical to the open-source logic provided in the official LoLo Logs library distribution.
